Clay gets everywhere, so plan for it.

## Clothes

Wear clothes you don't mind getting dirty. We lend aprons.

## Nails and jewelry

Long nails leave marks in the clay. Take off rings and watches.

## How long it takes

Your pieces need to dry and be fired twice, so they're ready about three weeks later.
