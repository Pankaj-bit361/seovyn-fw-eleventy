There are two main ways to shape clay.

## Hand-building

Pinching, coiling and slabs. You can make any shape, and it's forgiving for beginners.

## Wheel throwing

Shaping clay on a spinning wheel. Great for symmetrical pots, but centering the clay takes practice.

## Where to start

For a taster session, start with hand-building. Our six-week course covers both.
